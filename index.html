Worth knowing.

Plain answers about storage, calculations and using the app.

Is IronLog really free?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, paid tiers, feature gates or in-app purchases. The project is open source under the MIT licence.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, login or password. Open the app and start logging immediately.

Where is my workout data stored?

Workouts, custom exercises and settings are stored in your browser’s local storage. Nothing is sent to an IronLog server.

What happens if I clear browser data?

Your locally stored log is erased. Export a JSON backup from Settings first if you want to keep it or move it to another browser.

How is estimated one-rep max calculated?

IronLog uses the Epley formula: weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30). It is calculated only for completed sets with weight and at least one rep.

Can I use pounds instead of kilograms?

Yes. Choose kg or lb in Settings. Your logged values and records follow that unit preference.

Can I install IronLog on my phone?

Yes. IronLog includes a web app manifest and can be added to your home screen from a compatible browser. The app itself remains usable if the network drops after it has loaded.
